Nice Airport to Cannes: a fixed Standard fare, including festival periods

Cannes is one of the most booked road trips from Nice Côte d'Azur Airport. The town is about 29 km west of NCE. A Standard private transfer to Cannes starts from 86 € and usually takes about 35 minutes. The drive can also take around 30 to 40 minutes, depending on traffic.

The fare is for the vehicle, not per person. It includes motorway tolls, luggage assistance and waiting time. Once it is booked, a slow A8 does not become a supplement. This is a private chauffeur car, not an official taxi from the airport rank. The wider English site is on https://taxiaeroportnice.com/en/.

Festival periods and why booking ahead matters

Cannes is quiet on some weekdays and tightly packed on others. We publish a dedicated page, Cannes Film Festival Taxis and Airport Transfers, plus further English guides for MIPIM, Cannes Lions, the Yachting Festival and Canneseries. The practical point is simple: during festival periods it is unwise to rely on a spare car at the rank, and a pre booked driver with a name sign in arrivals is the more predictable option.

The Standard fare to Cannes remains from 86 €, including during festival periods. If you are travelling with colleagues or a film crew, book one van rather than two sedans, and give the venue entrance so the drop-off is at the right door.

Nearby towns often booked on the same trip include Antibes and Juan-les-Pins from 78 €, Mougins from 85 € and Mandelieu-la-Napoule from 94 €.

If your diary also includes a meeting in the principality, the Monaco route is a separate Standard fare, from 95 €, booked the same way. In both towns the price is agreed before you travel, and the driver is already waiting when you leave the hall. Give any access code or venue entrance in the booking comment, because Cannes hotels and palais drop-off points are not always obvious from the street.

How the pickup works

Give your flight number and the exact hotel, residence or venue address. The driver tracks the flight and waits in the arrivals hall of Terminal 1 or Terminal 2 with a name sign. At the airport, 60 minutes of waiting are free from the actual landing time. If you later leave Cannes for a departure, the return from Cannes to NCE uses the same Standard fare, from 86 €, with 20 minutes of waiting at the hotel or venue.

A sedan takes up to four passengers; a van takes up to eight, which is the usual choice for a crew or a family with a lot of luggage. Child seats are free if you give ages at booking. You pay the driver in cash or by card. Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before pickup. The service runs 24/7, including late landings the night before an event and very early runs back to the airport.
