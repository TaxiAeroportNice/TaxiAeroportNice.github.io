From Nice airport to the Pays de Fayence and the Dracénie

A practical guide to private transfers into the hilltop villages of the Var, written from the published fares and route pages.

Most people landing at Nice Côte d'Azur airport head for the coast. A smaller group turns inland, towards the perched villages above Lake Saint-Cassien and the market towns around Draguignan. Public transport there is thin, the roads climb, and a rental car after a long flight is not always what you want. This guide explains how a private transfer from NCE into the Pays de Fayence and the Dracénie works, what the published fares look like, and how to plan the return trip.

All prices below come from the public fare table on the tarifs page, checked on 20 November 2026 (Paris time). They are starting prices for a standard saloon, per one-way trip, and the page states that tolls, baggage and the personal greeting are included.

Montauroux, Fayence and Seillans: the first ridge

Montauroux is the closest of the group and the usual first stop for anyone staying near Lake Saint-Cassien. Fayence sits a few kilometres further along the same ridge, with its old village above the plain, and Seillans comes right after. The three are close to each other, so if your group is split between two rental houses, give both addresses when you ask for the price.

The fare page also names the Terre Blanche resort near Tourrettes and Fayence as a regular drop-off. If you are heading there, the Pays de Fayence transfer page gives an overview of the whole area, and the individual page for a private car to Seillans covers the last village on the ridge.

Draguignan, Flayosc and Lorgues: deeper into the Var

Past Seillans the landscape opens towards vineyards and the Dracénie, the area around Draguignan. Draguignan itself is the main town of the area and has its own route page. Flayosc and Lorgues sit just beyond it and are quieter, surrounded by estates. The fare page specifically mentions the Château de Berne near Lorgues and the Château de Rouët among the venues it serves.

For this part of the trip, the Dracénie route overview is the most useful page, and the Draguignan airport transfer page shows the town on its own. Journey times here depend more on the last few kilometres of country road than on the motorway, so the estimates in the table are a fair guide but not a promise to the minute.

Weddings, groups and luggage

The Pays de Fayence is a popular place for weddings in bastides and country houses, and the fare page says it handles guest transport for these events. For a group, the practical step is to send the list of flights and the venue address in one message, so the arrivals can be grouped where it makes sense. Vans are mentioned for bikes, golf bags and the cases of wine or olive oil that people tend to bring home from the Var. Child seats and boosters are listed as free on request.

What happens at the airport

Your driver follows the flight, so a delay does not mean a missed pickup. You are met with a name board as you come out of baggage claim at Terminal 1 or Terminal 2. Waiting time at the airport is free for 60 minutes, which covers most passport queues and slow baggage belts. The fare is fixed when you book and does not change because of traffic or a late landing.

Planning the trip back to NCE

The return works the same way in reverse and is priced per one-way trip. At a house, hotel or wedding venue the free waiting time is 20 minutes, so it helps to be ready at the agreed time. From Draguignan or Lorgues, allow for the full journey time in the table plus a margin for check-in. The reverse pages, such as the ride from Draguignan to Nice airport or Fayence to NCE, show the details for each village.

How to book

You can book online on the homepage at https://taxiaeroportnice.com, where the price is shown before you confirm. If your village is not in the table, the list of areas served is the next place to check, and a quote can be asked by phone, WhatsApp or email.
