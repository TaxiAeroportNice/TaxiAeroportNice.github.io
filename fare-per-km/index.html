Fare per kilometre from Nice airport: reading published prices side by side

A dated comparison of figures printed on public transfer websites, recorded on 20 November 2026 (Paris time).

This page asks one narrow question. When a transfer website around Nice Côte d'Azur airport (NCE) prints both a starting fare and a distance for the same destination, what does that fare come to per kilometre? It is plain arithmetic: each published price divided by the distance the same site shows next to it.

Six sites were read: https://taxiaeroportnice.com, vtcnice.eu, drive-me.cab, vtc06.com, taxilonguedistance.com and monaco-taxi-limousine.com. Every figure comes from their public pages as they stood on 20 November 2026. This is not a quote, a ranking or an opinion on service quality. Prices move, and the pages themselves stay the reference.

Where the numbers come from

  • taxiaeroportnice.com: the published fare table, lowest standard saloon price per route, with the distance printed in the same row. The page states that fares are fixed per one-way trip and include tolls, baggage and the greeting at arrivals.
  • vtcnice.eu: the homepage table, "Standard Car" column, with the distance shown in each row.
  • drive-me.cab: separate product pages for Antibes, Cannes, Monaco, Menton and Saint-Tropez, "Standard Sedan" option, with the distance given in the page text.
  • vtc06.com: the homepage table, which lists "dès" amounts and journey times but no distances.
  • monaco-taxi-limousine.com: the Monaco to Nice airport page, "Standard Car" row, with its own distance of 32.5 km.
  • taxilonguedistance.com: no route prices; the homepage gives an indicative long distance rule of 1.1 to 1.5 € per kilometre, tolls included, and invites a quote.

Only the lowest car category was taken from each site, so vans and premium classes stay out of the figures.

What the table shows

First, the sites do not print the same distance for the same place. Antibes is 24 km on one page, 20 km on another and 21 km on a third. Cannes ranges from 28 to 32 km, Saint-Tropez from 107 to 111 km, and no page says exactly where its distance is measured to. Since the fare is divided by that distance, a few kilometres either way on a short route shifts the result noticeably. The last column is a rough reading, not a precise metric.

Second, one pattern holds on every site: short trips cost more per kilometre than long ones. A run into Nice centre carries a fixed part (the greeting, the wait at arrivals, the drive back) spread over very few kilometres. On trips of 100 km or more, every published figure settles at around two euros per kilometre, or a little above.

Monaco, the one shared route

Monaco is the only destination where four of the six sites print both a distance and a price. It shows how close the figures sit on a busy route: starting fares between 90 € and 96 €, distances between 32 and 34 km. On taxiaeroportnice.com the transfer from NCE to Monaco is listed at 33 km.

Sites without a distance per route

vtc06.com lists starting amounts with journey times instead of kilometres, for example "dès 35 €" to Nice centre, "dès 60 €" to Antibes, "dès 70 €" to Cannes, "dès 75 €" to Grasse and "dès 90 €" to Monaco and to Menton. Its Saint-Tropez ("dès 180 €") and Fréjus / Saint-Raphaël ("dès 130 €") rows are labelled from Nice rather than from the airport. Without a published distance, a fare per kilometre would mean borrowing another site's figures, so it is left out of the calculation.

taxilonguedistance.com does not publish route prices for Nice. Its indicative rule of 1.1 to 1.5 € per kilometre is aimed at long trips, and its own table starts at 100 km. Applied to the two longest distances above, purely to show what the rule would give:

Limits of this reading

  • Distances belong to each site and are not measured from the same points.
  • "From" prices are starting prices. The final amount depends on the vehicle, the exact address and sometimes the time of day, as each site explains in its own terms.
  • Waiting time, inclusions and booking conditions differ and are not captured by a fare per kilometre. On taxiaeroportnice.com, waiting is free for 60 minutes at the airport and for 20 minutes at other pickup places.
  • All figures were read on 20 November 2026 and may have changed since.
